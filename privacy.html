Privacy Policy

Insure With Yuki respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, and insurance-related details when you contact us, submit a request, schedule a consultation, or request an insurance quote or coverage review.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your insurance inquiries, provide quote follow-ups, schedule appointments, conduct coverage reviews, provide policy service updates, and communicate with you regarding your request.

SMS Communications

By submitting your information through our website, scheduling a coverage review, or providing consent, you agree to receive one-to-one SMS messages from Insure With Yuki regarding your insurance inquiry.

SMS messages may include quote follow-ups, coverage review discussions, appointment scheduling, appointment reminders, and policy service updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for assistance.

SMS Privacy

Mobile opt-in data, SMS consent, and phone numbers collected for SMS communication purposes are not shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS consent is used only to communicate with customers regarding insurance inquiries, coverage reviews, appointment scheduling, quote follow-ups, and policy service requests.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as necessary to provide insurance-related services, comply with legal requirements, or support customer service operations.

This does not include mobile opt-in data or SMS consent, which will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.
