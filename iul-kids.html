IUL for kids and young adults

Many parents and grandparents want to do something special for the next generation. An Indexed Universal Life policy for a child or young adult can be one way to lock in early protection and long term savings in one place.

The idea is simple we start with a comfortable monthly amount and give the policy time to grow while they are young and healthy.

Schedule an IUL for kids consult

Owning a plan vs renting protection

With term life you are renting protection for a set period of time. With an Indexed Universal Life policy for a child you are building something they can keep and control later.

  • You lock in coverage while they are young and generally healthier.
  • Premiums are based on their age not yours so the cost can stay more manageable.
  • A portion of what you pay goes into a cash value bucket that can grow over time.

Why start early

Time is the biggest advantage. Starting a policy when they are young gives the cash value more years to grow and gives them more options later.

  • They can have coverage in place before health issues show up later in life.
  • Cash value has more time to build under the index based crediting.
  • Later they may be able to use the policy for goals like education a first home or retirement support while still keeping protection.

Example structure

Every plan is customized but a simple example could be something like two hundred dollars per month for a child or young adult depending on age health and goals.

  • We choose a starting monthly amount that fits your comfort level.
  • We design the policy so more of the payment supports long term cash value over time.
  • We review how the death benefit and the projected values could look at different ages.

These are not guarantees but a way to see how the plan might behave with different crediting scenarios and to decide if this strategy fits your family.

How we will set it up

During our meeting we will talk about who the policy is for what monthly amount feels comfortable and what you want this plan to do for them in the future.

  • We confirm health and basic information for the insured child or young adult.
  • We choose an initial funding level for the IUL and review sample illustrations.
  • We talk about how and when control of the policy can move to them when they are older.
