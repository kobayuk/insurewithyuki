Life insurance options

Life insurance is about more than a number on a page. It is about making sure your family has time space and money if something happens to you.

We can keep it simple or build something more advanced depending on your goals and your budget. Below are the four main types we can review together.

Book a life insurance review

Term life

Term life is simple protection for a set amount of time like 10 or 20 years. It is usually the lowest monthly cost.

  • Covers you for a set term while kids are growing or a mortgage is in place.
  • Higher coverage amounts can be more affordable than permanent life.
  • Good for income replacement and mortgage protection during your main working years.

Return of Premium term

Return of Premium term works like term life but if you outlive the term you can get close to your money back.

  • Protection during the term just like regular term life.
  • If you keep the policy to the end you can receive back most of what you paid in premiums.
  • Feels like a forced savings plan that can be used later for debt savings or another strategy.

Whole life

Whole life is permanent coverage with a fixed monthly payment and steady cash value growth.

  • Lifetime coverage as long as premiums are paid.
  • Premiums are designed to stay the same so it is easier to plan long term.
  • Cash value grows slowly and does not depend on the stock market.
  • Can help with final expenses or emergencies while still leaving a benefit to your family.

Indexed Universal Life

Indexed Universal Life is flexible life insurance with a savings bucket that can grow over time.

  • Flexible premium structure so you can increase or decrease funding within limits.
  • Cash value is tied to market indexes with protection on the downside so it is not invested directly in stocks.
  • Possible to use the cash value for big goals emergencies or retirement income later in life.
  • The life insurance can still pay the remaining amount to your family when you pass away.

During our appointment we can look at whether a simple term plan a Return of Premium option or a mix with cash value makes sense for you.

How we will review your options
