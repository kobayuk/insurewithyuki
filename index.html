Life insurance

We can look at different ways to protect your family and also build savings for later.

  • Term life
    10 or 20 year coverage with a simple death benefit and a lower monthly payment than permanent life insurance. This works well if you want to replace income or protect a mortgage during your main working years.
  • Return of Premium term
    If you outlive the term you can get close to your money back. It feels more like a forced savings plan with protection the whole time. At the end of the term you can use that money to help pay down debt add to savings or roll it into another plan.
  • Whole life
    Lifetime coverage with a fixed monthly payment and a cash value that grows slowly every year. This is good if you want a rate that does not change and a policy that will be there no matter how long you live. The cash value is steady and does not depend on the market. You can use it later for emergencies or final expenses and your family can still receive the remaining life insurance when you pass away.
  • Indexed Universal Life
    A flexible life policy with a savings bucket that can grow over time. The cash value is linked to market indexes with protection on the downside so it can grow without direct market loss. You can use the cash value later for emergencies big goals or retirement income and the life insurance can still pay the remaining amount to your family if something happens to you.

During our appointment we can see which option or mix of options fits your budget today and what you want this money to do in the future.
