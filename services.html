Insurance services

Auto home life and commercial insurance all work together. We can keep everything under one roof so you have one main contact instead of different 800 numbers.

Below is a quick overview of the main areas I help with. We can go over your current policies or start from the beginning if you prefer a fresh set of eyes.

Schedule a coverage review

Auto coverage

We can review your limits and deductibles so you are not underinsured or overpaying.

  • Liability limits for injuries and damage to others.
  • Comprehensive and collision to protect your own car when needed.
  • Uninsured and underinsured motorist for drivers who do not carry enough coverage.
  • Options to adjust deductibles and structure your premium around your comfort level.

Home and property

Your home is often your biggest asset. We will make sure the coverage matches real rebuild costs and water protection needs.

  • Coverage for the structure and attached features.
  • Personal property protection for what you own inside the home.
  • Liability if someone is hurt on your property.
  • Options for water leak detection and added protection for modern claims.

Life and income protection

Life insurance can help your family keep the house stay on track with goals and have time to breathe if something happens to you.

  • Term life for straightforward income and mortgage protection.
  • Return of Premium term when you want close to your money back if you outlive the term.
  • Whole life and Indexed Universal Life for longer term and cash value strategies.

We can also look at options for kids or young adults if you want to start early planning.

Business and commercial coverage

For small businesses and self employed professionals we can build a simple plan that covers the main risks without overcomplicating things.

  • Commercial auto for work vehicles and small fleets.
  • Business owners policies for offices shops and salons.
  • General liability when a landlord or client requests proof of insurance.
  • Workers compensation when you have employees on payroll.

Bring your current policies or we can design a structure from scratch that fits how your business actually runs day to day.
